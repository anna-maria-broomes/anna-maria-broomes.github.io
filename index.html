Calavoix

I founded Calavoix, an independent venture based in Montréal. We develop analytical instruments for governance, decision systems, and operational intelligence.

Calavoix grew out of my doctoral research into the gap between how organizations structure their operations and how work truly unfolds. That research led me toward computational methods, evidence architecture, and the design of systems that make complex organizational behaviour observable.

Background

My work draws on applied research in complex workplace settings, including maritime and unionized operations. I also bring expertise in psychotherapy, university teaching, and facilitating leadership development.

Supervised clinical practice in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) gave me a deep understanding of psychological flexibility, decision-making, mindfulness, and how individual experience relates to larger systems.
